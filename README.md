    
🎬 Netflix Dataset Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a Netflix customer and content dataset to understand customer behavior, subscription patterns, viewing activity, revenue-related metrics, and content preferences.

The project uses Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI/Tableau to clean, analyze, and visualize the data and generate meaningful business insights.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and subscription plans
- Identify active and inactive customers
- Understand customer viewing behavior
- Analyze movies and TV shows watched by customers
- Identify popular genres and content preferences
- Analyze monthly sessions and watch hours
- Compare subscription plans and monthly fees
- Analyze customer ratings and download activity
- Create interactive dashboards for business reporting

📊 Dataset Information

The dataset contains customer, subscription, and viewing-related information.

Key Columns

Column| Description
Customer_ID| Unique customer identifier
Customer_Name| Customer name
Age| Customer age
Title| Movie or TV show title
Content_Type| Movie or TV Show
Genre| Content genre
Country| Customer country
Subscription_Plan| Subscription plan
Device| Device used for viewing
Payment_Method| Customer payment method
Account_Status| Active/Inactive account status
Join_Date| Customer registration date
Last_Watch_Date| Most recent viewing date
Watch_Hours| Total viewing hours
Monthly_Fees| Monthly subscription fee
Movies_Watched| Number of movies watched
Episodes_Watched| Number of episodes watched
Avg_Rating| Average customer rating
Monthly_Sessions| Number of monthly sessions
Download_Count| Number of downloaded contents
